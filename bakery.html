Our Story

Baking is our passion,
not just our craft.

La Miette began in a small kitchen in 2009, born from a simple belief: that good bread changes a morning, and a great pastry can make a whole day. We source the finest local flour, seasonal fruits, and free-range eggs to create goods that nourish both body and soul.

Our Process

Source

Ferment

Shape
